# Landing-page visual direction

The brief called for a stronger color scheme and less generic visual styling while retaining the compact homepage and actual student-world preview.

## References inspected

- [Pentagram — The New School](https://www.pentagram.com/work/the-new-school/story): expressive sans-serif typography, a disciplined red/black identity, and rules that organize content. The original proprietary typeface and logo are not reproduced.
- [Wolff Olins — The Met](https://wolffolins.com/work/the-met): a recognizable cultural identity with strong typography, contrast, and imagery. This informed the degree of visual confidence, not a copy of its logo or page.

## Applied system

- White `#ffffff`, ink `#171717`, vermilion `#d8321c`, and neutral gray `#595959`.
- Vermilion has approximately 4.78:1 contrast on white; the darker `#bd2816` supports smaller catalog links.
- Helvetica Neue / Helvetica / Arial typography, strong headline weight, sharp edges, and visible section rules.
- Numbered lesson rows replace pastel icon tiles. The actual recorded student-world image is retained without recoloring.
- The shared lesson picker uses the same light theme from every entry point. Public pages reuse `PageHeader` and `Brand`; the studio and student world use a charcoal variant of the same identity.
- Mobile layout stacks the preview, retains readable labels and focus indicators, and has no horizontal overflow at the checked 390-pixel viewport.
