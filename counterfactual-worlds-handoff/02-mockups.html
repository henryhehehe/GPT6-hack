Counterfactual Worlds

Lesson plan in, playable world out. These are the screens and the flow Astra should build toward. Wireframes are proportional; copy is real content from the Alexandria hero lesson.

Runtime three.js · browser
Model gpt-6-astra
Budget 13 h · team ≤ 4
Fidelity low-poly diorama

1 · How a lesson becomes a world

Generation happens once, at authoring time. Everything the student touches is cached. The only live model call during class is the teacher's director patch, which is a diff — not a regen.

2 · The three screens

Teacher authoring, student world, teacher director board. Each is one screen — no navigation, no settings pages.

generated by Astra / live modelcounterfactual delta · director actioncached world geometry

3 · What the model emits vs. what the runtime draws

The contract that keeps the demo safe. Astra writes declarative primitives; the runtime owns the meshes. Five structure kinds cover a Hellenistic city, a market square, or a physics playground.

4 · Demo storyboard · 3 minutes

Three live beats, three from video. Every beat is something an investor can see from the back of the room without reading a word.

0:00 – 0:20 · live

Paste-to-world reveal streams

Wireframe footprints fill in as JSON tokens arrive; walls rise a second later. Build incrementally — never wait for the full spec.

0:20 – 1:00 · video

Two worlds, one lever

Same street grid; toggle the lever and watch buildings and ships appear. The thesis with no narration.

1:00 – 2:00 · live

The argument opens a door model call

Argument quality made physical. Weak claim, shut door. Structural claim with evidence, the librarian concedes and the archive unlocks.

2:00 – 2:40 · live

Heatmap to patch director

"Give the merchant a ledger." Cut to a student screen: scroll on the table, merchant turns. Under ten seconds, round trip.

2:40 – 2:50 · video

Thirty students, thirty framings

Courier, cook, dockhand, scribe — one world, personalized framing per student. Personalization at a glance, free to render.

2:50 – 3:00 · video · if time

Break the universe

Ten seconds of the same engine running a physics lesson. Says "platform," then stop talking.

Visual direction for the runtime. Flat-shaded low-poly, one warm directional light plus soft fog, a limestone-and-Aegean palette; the counterfactual delta is always amber so the audience can find it. Camera: third-person isometric orbit, click-to-move. Target 5–8 structures, 3–4 NPCs, 30–60 s of walking. Anything beyond this is cut.
