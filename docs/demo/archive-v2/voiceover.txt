What if a history lesson became a place you could investigate? This is Counterfactual Worlds.

The teacher starts with a prepared Alexandria lesson. Students explore the city, then change one assumption: what if harbor trade collapsed?

A student predicts the library will definitely close. Astra challenges that certainty and asks what the evidence supports.

The teacher adds a new challenge: could another patron support the scholars? We preview the question, then add it to the same student world.

Students read Strabo inside the app, distinguish historical evidence from invented teaching props, and cite the source.

The revised explanation connects trade, income, and scholar support, while recognizing that another patron could change the outcome.

Counterfactual Worlds makes reasoning visible.
