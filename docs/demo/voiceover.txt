“The library will definitely close.” That’s the first answer. Let’s test it.

Counterfactual Worlds turns history and literature into investigations students can walk through.

Teachers choose from thirty prepared lessons, review the sources, and get a teaching guide.

In Alexandria, we collapse harbor trade. Fewer ships. Less income. But must the scholars disappear?

Students read Strabo, cite the evidence, and defend their explanation. Astra challenges unsupported certainty.

Then the teacher changes the challenge: could a new patron help? Preview. Apply. The student keeps their work.

Now the answer changes: “Closure is possible, not inevitable.” The teacher can review the first and latest explanations side by side.
