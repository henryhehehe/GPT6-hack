# Visual and sound direction — version 5

Reference research used the official [Screen Studio product page](https://screen.studio/), its [animation guide](https://preview.screen.studio/guide/animations), and [Linear’s introductory lesson](https://linear.app/learn/intro-to-linear). These pages informed the approach; no footage, soundtrack, or proprietary assets were copied.

The story begins with a concrete mistaken claim, shows the tools used to investigate it, then returns to the revised explanation. Teacher and student labels make role changes clear. The edit uses 200 ms dissolves and one-second eased pushes toward the active panel, with restrained movement on city views. Labeled excerpts make the actual writing readable without replacing the interface. The last seven seconds settle into the product and closing line.

**A Question Opens** is an original 60-second instrumental composed locally for this film: warm synth pads, gentle keys, bass, and a sparse pulse at 80 BPM. It uses synthesized tones, no downloaded music or samples. Its source is `scripts/demo/music-v5.py`; provenance is saved beside the WAV. Music is normalized below the voice, automatically ducked during speech, and faded at the ends. The narration remains conversational OpenAI Marin speech.
