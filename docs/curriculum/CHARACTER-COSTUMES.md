# Character clothing: evidence and interpretation

The reading companions are fictional, stylized teaching figures. Clothing references support broad period silhouettes, not a historical person's identity, status, ethnicity, biography or exact outfit. The models are original geometry, not downloaded museum reproductions. Museum images have not been copied into the assets. Dialogue remains bounded by the assigned source packet.

`lib/characterCostumes.ts` is the explicit work-to-model registry. It replaces the previous hashed assignment of the same three nineteenth-century readers across all post-ancient settings. The companion menu exposes each interpretation and its references under **About the clothing**. Custom worlds explicitly retain unverified illustrative clothing.

## Authoring and verification

The fifteen new models reuse the original face, hand, skin-weight and animation helpers with five separately authored clothing families. Each has Idle, Greeting and Talk clips. All are original MIT-licensed interpretive assets with editable Blender sources recorded in the manifest. They remain stylized; fabrics, hair, footwear and construction details are simplified, and costume expertise could refine them further.

Regenerate with the installed Blender binary and `--background --factory-startup --python scripts/blender/build_period_characters.py`. An optional `-- medieval`, `-- earlymodern`, `-- georgian`, `-- regency`, or `-- romantic` regenerates a single family. `scripts/blender/render_period_characters.py` produces back and greeting pose checks. Five neutral, five back and five greeting studio renders were inspected; the medieval girdles and Regency waist ribbons were moved outside the cloth after the first inspection revealed intersections.

All 52 authored models pass the existing size, checksum, bounds, material, triangle, source, rig and clip checks. The 21 wardrobe models referenced by the cast registry additionally pass finite geometry/animation checks, independent-skeleton checks and loader normalization. The full authored catalog is 16,483,616 bytes, but a world requests only three reader models. The unchanged 15 MB scene budget is now measured conservatively as all props, all background citizens, both legacy landmarks and the three largest readers: 11,029,916 bytes. External scene assets are outside this authored-model budget and retain their own checks.

Browser integration and device performance have not been visually tested in this pass. Offline studio pose inspection does not establish in-world lighting quality, accessibility or historical reconstruction accuracy.
